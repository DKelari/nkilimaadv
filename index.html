02

Biography

Naomi Kajaira Kilima is an accomplished Advocate of the High Court of Kenya and a Partner at Agoi Kilima & Company Advocates, with over a decade of experience in legal practice. She advises and represents corporate entities, private clients, and non-governmental organisations across diverse areas of law, combining strategic legal expertise with a strong commitment to access to justice, human rights, and social justice.

Naomi has extensive experience working with NGOs and private clients, delivering impactful legal services in litigation, legal aid, family and children’s law, human rights, and other areas of legal practice. Her work is driven by a commitment to protecting vulnerable and marginalised communities, particularly women and children, while advancing accountability and equitable access to justice.

She has contributed significantly to the legal profession through her service on the Law Society of Kenya’s Gender Committee, Child Law Practitioners Committee, and Legal Aid and Awareness Committee, championing gender equality, child protection, and public legal education. She is also a Court-Annexed Mediator, supporting alternative dispute resolution and the efficient administration of justice.

A passionate legal educator and commentator, Naomi has featured on KTN, Classic FM, and other local media platforms, where she provides legal insights and promotes public awareness of legal rights, court processes, and access to justice.

03

Practice areas

  1. Legal counsel on divorce, child custody, guardianship, adoption, matrimonial property division, succession, trusts, and estate planning.

  2. Mediation of commercial and personal disputes, including sessions conducted by Google Meet.

  3. Advice and representation on contracts, compliance, and civil and commercial proceedings in Kenya.

  4. Legal advisory and representation on constitutional rights, human rights violations, access to justice, legal aid, gender equality, and child protection. Supporting NGOs, institutions, and individuals in promoting accountability, protecting fundamental freedoms, and advancing social justice through litigation, advocacy, and public legal awareness.

  5. Providing legal representation and advisory services in criminal proceedings, including watching briefs in sexual offences cases to safeguard victims’ rights and interests. Advocating for the constitutional rights of accused persons, particularly in cases involving violations of due process, unlawful arrest or detention, and infringement of fair trial rights.

  6. Providing legal services in property transactions, including sale and purchase agreements, title transfers, leases, property due diligence, land registration, and completion of conveyancing transactions. Advising clients on property ownership, compliance, and the protection of their interests throughout the transaction process.

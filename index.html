02

Biography

Naomi Kajaira Kilima is an accomplished Advocate of the High Court of Kenya and a Partner at Agoi Kilima & Company Advocates, with over a decade of experience in legal practice. She advises and represents corporate entities, private clients, and non-governmental organisations across diverse areas of law, combining strategic legal expertise with a strong commitment to access to justice, human rights, and social justice.

Naomi has extensive experience working with NGOs and private clients, delivering impactful legal services in litigation, legal aid, family and children’s law, human rights, and other areas of legal practice. Her work is driven by a commitment to protecting vulnerable and marginalised communities, particularly women and children, while advancing accountability and equitable access to justice.

She has contributed significantly to the legal profession through her service on the Law Society of Kenya’s Gender Committee, Children and the Law Committee, and Legal Awareness Committee, championing gender equality, child protection, and public legal education. She is also a Court-Annexed Mediator, supporting alternative dispute resolution and the efficient administration of justice.

A passionate legal educator and commentator, Naomi has featured on KTN, Classic FM, and other local media platforms, where she provides legal insights and promotes public awareness of legal rights, court processes, and access to justice.
